About PropertyWale

PropertyWale is a cloud-based property search and management portal built to make it simple to find, compare and enquire about flats, villas, plots, shops and commercial spaces across India — without the noise of unverified listings and broker spam.

This build stage focuses on the public search experience: browsing by category, filtering by city / locality / budget, and viewing full property details. Owner accounts, an admin dashboard, and enquiry routing are on the roadmap for the next stages of the project.
